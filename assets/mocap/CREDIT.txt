The golf swing and putt are takes 64_06 (swing) and 64_13 (putt) from the
Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, subject 64.

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The
database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

CMU places no restrictions on use in research or commercial products.
BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed.com, 2010 MotionBuilder-friendly
release). Frame 0 of each file is a T-pose added by that conversion, not
part of the captured swing. No other frames were edited.
